## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a **Minimalist Editorial + Swiss/Grid-based** philosophy infused with retro-modern graphic design accents and playful geometric motifs. Designed specifically for a Grade 12 Visual Communication Design (DKV) student, the system projects an aesthetic that is bold, creative, warm, youthful, yet deeply disciplined in typographic rigor and layout structure.

It eschews generic tech SaaS conventions, glassmorphism, and neon gradients in favor of tactile color blocking, fine editorial hairline dividers, prominent condensed display lettering, and physical print-inspired warmth. The interface feels like a high-end physical graphic design publication brought to life on the web—celebrating student craftsmanship, curiosity, and structured visual storytelling.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in an earthy, warm chromatic harmony composed of four distinct actors:
- **Primary (`#411F10`)**: Deep Chocolate Brown serves as the foundational anchor, delivering sharp editorial text contrast, structured hairline rules, and grounded layout containers.
- **Secondary (`#FFCA00`)**: Warm Vivid Yellow commands high visual energy, used for major featured backdrops (such as curated project showcases), graphic badges, sunburst motifs, and primary highlight accents.
- **Tertiary (`#4A87AA`)**: Muted Dusty Blue acts as an editorial cooling agent, framing photography, housing secondary tool showcases, and grounding key category pills.
- **Neutral (`#FBF6EA`)**: Warm Cream forms the global canvas, deliberately avoiding stark sterile whites to preserve a tactile, risograph or coated-paper printed sensibility.

### Contrast & Hierarchy Rules
- Never use Vivid Yellow (`#FFCA00`) for fine body typography or critical informational text against Cream or White; use it strictly as a container surface or graphic accent with Dark Chocolate typography layered on top.
- Surface White (`#FFFFFF`) is reserved for foreground cards and high-contrast content modules.
- Borders and hairline rules strictly use `#411F10` at low thicknesses (1px) or subtle opacity steps.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional tension between monumental condensed letterforms and highly legible, utilitarian sans-serif typography.

### Heading System (Bebas Neue)
Dominant editorial headings utilize a towering condensed display font. It brings heroic presence without demanding excessive vertical height, allowing titles like "PORTFOLIO" to span full widths while preserving tight leading (0.85–0.95em).

### Body & Structural Text (Inter)
Inter manages editorial narrative copy, project subtitles, and micro-labels. Small uppercase labels are heavily tracked (`+0.04em` to `+0.08em`) to evoke Swiss modernist poster compositions, museum catalogs, and identity manuals.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an uncompromising 8px spatial rhythm within a max-width 1200px container (`75rem`), balancing tight graphic alignments with generous breathing room.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (≥1024px)**: 12-column grid structure with 24px gutters. Hero compositions use asymmetric multi-layered elements; Selected Works display a rigorous 4-column single-tier card row; Tools and Process divide cleanly in a split modular arrangement.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column layout. Selected Works reflow into a 2x2 symmetrical grid. Hero and About Me collapse into balanced vertical segments.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column layout with 16px lateral padding. All horizontal sequences (process milestones, cards, tool clusters) convert into stacked vertical flows while preserving full-width graphic impact.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately rejects skeumorphic drop-shadows and blurred glassmorphism, relying instead on **Graphic Layering, Border Structures, and Strict Color-Blocking**:

- **Depth through Layering:** Depth is communicated purely by overlapping flat elements (e.g., circular sunburst decals positioned behind portrait frames, or stats bars that ground photographic imagery).
- **Hairline Framing:** Crisp 1px solid borders in `#411F10` separate layered surfaces, giving elements physical structural presence.
- **Micro-Interaction Elevation:** On active hover states for interactive project cards, a restrained physical lift is applied: `transform: translateY(-3px)` combined with an ambient, low-spread grounding shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px rgba(65, 31, 16, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The geometric vernacular combines controlled, humanistic rounded rectangles with exact geometric primitives:
- **Cards & Content Blocks:** Set to standard rounded borders (`rounded-md` to `rounded-lg`, 8px–12px) to keep content grounded without looking overly bubbly.
- **Badges & Interactive Tags:** Configured as pure pills (`rounded-full`, 9999px) to contrast against sharp divider lines and geometric photographs.
- **Decorative Primitives:** Perfect circles, quadrant crops, and 8-point geometric stars/sparkles represent student creativity and graphic play.

## Components

### Buttons & Editorial Pills
- **Pill Badge:** Height 32px–36px, `border-radius: 9999px`, padding `6px 16px`. Default style: Muted Dusty Blue (`#4A87AA`) or Deep Brown (`#411F10`) with Warm Cream text, uppercase tracking `0.06em`.
- **Action Pill / Button:** Warm Vivid Yellow (`#FFCA00`) with Deep Chocolate Brown text (`#411F10`) and a 1px border (`#411F10`). Hover triggers a subtle color shift and 1px translateY offset.

### Project Showcase Cards
- **Container:** Background `#FFFFFF` or `#FBF6EA`, 1px solid border (`#411F10`), 12px border radius, 16px internal padding.
- **Media Wrapper:** 4:3 or 16:10 aspect ratio container with 8px inner border radius, hidden overflow.
- **Card Metadata:** Includes an index pill (e.g., `01`), category caption in tracked uppercase `Inter`, followed by a bold project title.

### Section Dividers & Graphic Accents
- **Editorial Hairlines:** 1px horizontal rule (`#411F10`) spanning layouts, frequently interrupted by a centered decorative star (`✦`) or miniature sun icon.
- **Process Sequence:** 5-step milestone chain connected by horizontal line rails, each step anchored by a circular numbered badge (36px diameter) and concise description.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Flat cream background (`#FBF6EA`), 1px solid border (`#411F10`), 8px border radius, 12px 16px padding. Active focus states emit a 2px solid ring in `#411F10` without glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp or soft-rounded 18px boxes with 1.5px `#411F10` border and solid `#411F10` fill upon selection.
