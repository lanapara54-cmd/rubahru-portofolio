MY DESIGN PROCESS

01

🧭 Explore & Research

Understand the brief, brand, and audience before any pixel is placed.

02

✏️ Wireframe & Sketch

Low-fi sketches that map the structure before the fun colors arrive.

03

🎨 Design & Style

Build layout, type, and palette — calm waves, tidy shores.

04

🧪 Prototype & Review

Bring it to life, gather feedback, and iterate with a happy dance.

05

🚀 Deliver & Support

Export clean files, hand off, and support the brand way beyond the first launch.
